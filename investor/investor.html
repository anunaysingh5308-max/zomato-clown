Key Takeaways

Here are the key highlights from Zomato's Q3FY25 shareholders' letter and results.

01

Strong Topline Growth

Adjusted Revenue grew 58% year-over-year and 12% quarter-over-quarter to ₹5,746 crore.

02

Adjusted EBITDA Growth

Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA increased 128% YoY to ₹285 crore, supported largely by stronger food delivery margins.

03

Quick Commerce Investment

Quarterly losses increased as the company accelerated investments in expanding the quick commerce store network.

04

Blinkit Expansion

Blinkit crossed the 1,000-store mark one quarter ahead of plan, with a target of 2,000 stores by December 2025.

05

Food Delivery Performance

Food delivery GOV grew 17% YoY, while Adjusted EBITDA margins improved from 3.5% to 4.3%.

06

New Mobile Apps

Zomato launched the all-new District and Bistro mobile applications.
